Security without false promises

No system is impossible to attack. A good system is small, separated, auditable, and recoverable.

Minimize

Static files, no trackers, no third-party JavaScript, no secrets, no server-side execution.

Separate

Website, wallet signer, experiment controller, and robot controller remain independent.

Verify

Signed releases, hashes, protected deployment credentials, raw telemetry, and immutable logs.
